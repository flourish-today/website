Setup

You'll need ADB on a computer or on the phone itself, and no accounts or users on the phone while you set the device owner. You can add accounts and user profiles back afterwards.

FAQ

Does Beeswax work on my phone?

Beeswax has been tested on the stock Pixel OS, GrapheneOS, and HyperOS.

Do I need a computer or a factory reset?

You need ADB once, during setup. You can use a computer with wireless debugging, USB debugging, or on your phone directly with Termux. Android only lets Beeswax manage a phone with no accounts, no other user profiles and no Private Spaces, so you'll remove those first. A factory reset always works if that's easier.

What if I forget my lock code?

Without your lock code, you can't access new apps you install and can't access apps you've blocked. You can still export all your data and access the apps you already have. If you enabled Recovery wait in More settings beforehand, tap Lost code to start the wait. When it ends, tap Unlock to open the list without your code. Set a new code before closing the list. The wait uses days, hours and minutes; setting all three to zero turns it off. Restarts can make the wait longer, and time with the phone off does not count. Without a recovery wait, you'll need to factory reset your phone.

What doesn't work with Beeswax?
  • You can't use Private Spaces. Create other users through Beeswax, rather than Android Settings.
  • Links that would open in a browser don't open while Beeswax is locked, unless you've allowed a browser.
  • Apps with a built-in browser, like a help link or an in-app browser, may still open websites. Choose the apps you keep with that in mind.
How do I remove Beeswax?

Enter your lock code, tap Unlock, then tap Deactivate and confirm. Then you can uninstall it.

What if I have an iPhone or iPad?

You should use Apple Configurator (guide1 / guide2) to manage your devices, remove the browser and prevent new app installs. A macOS device and a factory reset of your iPhone/iPad are required for the initial setup.
